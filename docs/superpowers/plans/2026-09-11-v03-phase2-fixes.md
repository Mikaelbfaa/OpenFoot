# v0.3 Phase 2 Fix Wave Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Close the defects the final whole-branch review of the phase 2 season core found, so the season obeys spec sections 0, 1.9, 1.10, 1.11, 1.12, 3.1 and 3.8 and the FORMAT-SPEC .ces rules before evolution and youth build on it.

**Architecture:** Seven sequential tasks over `engine/src/main/kotlin/org/openfoot/engine/season/`, the dataset options, the importer and the CLI. Membership that the spec says persists across seasons (the country reserve queue, the state divisions and state reserves, the clubs at the door of the Brazilian fourth division) moves onto `SeasonState`. Each task states its rulings. The tasks need design judgement, so they give exact requirements and test cases rather than complete code.

**Tech Stack:** Kotlin/JVM 21, Gradle, kotlin.test.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-09-v03-temporada-design.md`, with `spec/SIMULATION-SPEC.md` and `spec/FORMAT-SPEC.md` as the binding text. The findings come from the final review recorded in `.superpowers/sdd/2026-09-10-v03-phase2-season-core/progress.md`.

### Task 1: discipline per competition, served before the cards, and every Sunday of the year

**Findings closed:**
- Critical 1: suspensions are served in the round they are earned.
- The per-competition record of 3.8.
- Important 9: Sundays after the last match never fire.
- Deferred minors T8: the dropped per-competition guard and the unchecked `ClubState` cast.
- Item 116's misreading of 1.4.

**Files:** `ClubState.kt`, `RoundLoop.kt`, `Turnover.kt` (discipline reset only), tests in `engine/src/test/kotlin/org/openfoot/engine/season/`, `spec/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md` item 116, the CLI golden vector.

**Rulings:**
1. Spec 3.8 keeps the card record per competition: "o teste que roda ao fim de cada partida do clube do jogador nessa competição".
   - `PlayerRecord.discipline` becomes a map from competition key to `DisciplineRecord`. An absent key means `DisciplineRecord.CLEAN`.
   - A player is available for a match of competition C when he is not injured on that date and not suspended in C.
   - A league ban never keeps a man out of a cup match.
2. Spec 3.1's post-round order is "cumpre suspensões" before "aplica notas/cartões". At the end of each match a club plays in competition C, for that club:
   - first, every record of the club suspended in C serves one match, through `DisciplineRecord.served()`, which already applies the yellows-first rule of 3.8;
   - then the match's cards are applied to C's records through the existing per-side discipline stream.

   The separate serve step of `postRound` goes away. A club with no match in C that day serves nothing in C (unchanged, item 117). Recovery is unchanged.
3. Spec section 0: no Sunday is skipped. After the season's last scheduled date, `playSeason` fires every Sunday still pending through 31 December of the season's year, in order, before it returns.
4. The card record belongs to a competition instance, and every competition is rebuilt at the turnover, so the next season starts with empty discipline maps. Item 116 is rewritten to say:
   - 1.4's per-club reset is a cache resync, not a zeroing of statistics;
   - the clean records follow from the per-competition-instance record (INFERIDO).

   Injuries still carry by date.
5. `playRound` again requires that a club appears at most once within one competition's round. This is besides the existing guard against a club in two competitions on one date. `availabilityOf` looks the club up in the current state by key instead of casting the competitor.

**Tests (write first, see them fail):**
- A third yellow in league match k: the player is unavailable for the club's league match k+1 and available for k+2. The same player is available for a cup match between them.
- A two-match direct red ban in the league is served only by league matches. A cup match in between does not reduce it.
- Two yellows in the league plus one in the cup does not suspend, because the records are per competition.
- A club on a bye in C serves nothing in C that day.
- A counting `WeeklyTick` over a whole season fires exactly once per Sunday from the season start through 31 December of the year. Count the Sundays with `CalendarDate`.
- A club scheduled twice in one competition's round is refused with a message naming the club and the competition.

**Golden vector:** `SeasonGoldenVectorTest` moves because bans now cost matches. Re-pin it and argue it in the commit body.

**Commit:** `fix(engine): suspensions are kept per competition and served before the match's cards`

### Task 2: a calendar that holds every league the schema allows, or refuses it by name

**Findings closed:**
- Critical 2: a league above 38 rounds aborts the season.
- Deferred minors T7:
  - `Slot` has no docstring;
  - `roundCount` duplicates the competition's own leg count;
  - one clause of the require is always true;
  - the overrun and the state boundary have no tests.
- The calendar constants' SpecRefs.
- The wording of items 110 and 111.

**Files:** `Schedule.kt`, `Competition.kt` (one source for a competition's total dated rounds), tests, `spec/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md` items 110 and 111.

**Rulings:**
1. Spec 1.10: every national league of the world shares the same "Nacional" dates.
   - The league date list is the Sundays from week 12 through the last Sunday of the year.
   - When the longest active league needs more rounds than those Sundays, the extra rounds take the Wednesdays of weeks with no cup date, from week 12 onward in chronological order.
   - The merged list is kept in date order.
   - Each league plays its rounds on the first R dates of that shared list, R being its own round count.
2. A league whose round count exceeds the Sundays plus the free Wednesdays is refused before the season starts. The message names the competition, the rounds it needs and the dates available.
3. The number of dated rounds of a competition is computed in one place, on `Competition` or its phases, and the schedule reads it. The schedule and the close must agree by construction.
4. The calendar constants and the open questions:
   - The calendar constants keep a SpecRef on the section they serve, 1.10 or 0. Their docstrings say the value is item 110's bet.
   - Item 110 records rules 1 and 2 as INFERIDO.
   - Items 110 and 111 drop the verb "restaura".
   - Item 111's heading names the construction the code uses, the Latin square over group pairs, not the rejected circle with dropped pairs.

**Tests:**
- For every club count from 10 to 36 and every turn count from 1 to 4, a single active league either gets a valid schedule or is refused with the named message. A valid schedule has strictly increasing dates, all inside the year, none on a cup date. No other exception escapes.
- A 22-club two-turn league (42 rounds) and a 24-club two-turn league (46 rounds) fit, and their extra dates are Wednesdays without a cup round.
- A state competition that would pass week 12 is refused by name.
- The year-overrun refusal fires for a shape beyond capacity.

**Acceptance (local, nothing committed):**

2. Run `openfoot-cli season --dataset <base.json from the real install> --seed 42 --seasons 2 --leagues BRA,ESP` and check that it completes.

### Task 3: the country reserve is a queue carried across seasons

**Findings closed:**
- Important 3: a short reserve relegates the best of the zone.
- Important 4: the reserve is re-sorted by level every season.
- Deferred minor T9: `MAX_QUEUE_DEPTH`'s loose SpecRef.

**Files:** `SeasonState.kt`, `Turnover.kt`, `NationalLeagues.kt` if needed, tests.

**Rulings:**
1. Spec 1.12: the last division swaps with the country reserve.
   - Out go the `min(nRebaixados, reserve size)` LAST clubs of the final order.
   - In come as many clubs from the HEAD of the reserve queue.
   - The relegated join the TAIL of the queue in their final order, best first.
   - There is no new level reading after world creation.
2. `SeasonState` carries the reserve queue of every country with an active league. The opening season seeds it from the pyramid's own order of the clubs it left without a division (1.9: by level, with the per-club tiebreak the world generation already drew). The queue is data on the state, not recomputed.
3. Clubs leaving the Brazilian fourth division at its rebuild join the tail of Brazil's reserve queue.
4. `MAX_QUEUE_DEPTH` cites the FORMAT-SPEC state preset table its value follows.

**Tests:**
- A reserve of one club: the last-placed club goes down and the second-last stays up.
- A strong relegated club sits at the tail of the queue. It is not promoted back the next season while the queue holds clubs ahead of it.
- Three seasons of a two-division country with a reserve: every club has exactly one standing each season, and division sizes never change.

**Commit:** `fix(engine): the country reserve is a queue carried from season to season`

### Task 4: state divisions persist, relegate and promote, and the fourth division is built from this season's states

**Findings closed:**
- Important 5: state relegation and the state reserve swap are missing.
- Important 7: the short state queue is not padded.
- The season-one fourth division is seated by level, against 1.9.
- The truthfulness of items 113, 121 and 122.
- The invented purpose of `StateSetup.reserve`.

**Files:** `SeasonState.kt`, `StateChampionships.kt`, `Turnover.kt`, `RoundLoop.kt` (building the fourth when the states close), `Schedule.kt` if needed, tests, `spec/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md` items 113, 121 and 122 and a new item 123.

**Rulings:**
1. FORMAT-SPEC, .ces "Rebaixados e promovidos", at the end of a season:
   - Each state division relegates the last `nRebaixados` clubs, the preset's count, of the first phase's overall table, not per group.
   - The division below promotes as many clubs, by merit order.
   - The last state division swaps with the state reserve as a queue: the last min(relegated, reserve size) clubs go out, the reserve head comes in, and the relegated go to the tail.

   `SeasonState` carries every state's division memberships and reserve queue. Season one seeds them from `stateSetup`, and later seasons build the state competitions from the carried memberships.
2. The order of a carried membership matters because grouped presets deal it k mod groups. The order is the division's kept clubs in their previous membership order, then the arrivals in merit order. This is a bet, recorded as new item 123 (INFERIDO).
3. Spec 1.9 and 1.12: with states active, Brazil's fourth division is not filled by level. It is built from THIS season's state champions' queue.
   - The league competition for the fourth is created when the season's last state competition closes, before the league start.
   - Its queue has three parts, in order:
     - the clubs the third relegated at the previous turnover (none in season one);
     - this season's state queue, in item 81's order;
     - Brazilian clubs with no state division, in world order.
   - Any club in divisions one to three, and any club already chosen, is skipped.
   - The list stops at the configured size, or when candidates run out.
   - The schedule reserves the fourth's dates from its configured shape. A built fourth whose shape differs from the reserved one is refused by name.
4. At the turnover:
   - `SeasonState` holds the third's relegated clubs as the fourth's door for the next season.
   - The fourth's best clubs, as many as the third relegates, still go up by the fourth's final order (item 121).
   - Everyone else of the old fourth joins the tail of Brazil's reserve queue (Task 3).
5. Items rewritten:
   - Item 113 acknowledges 1.9 and records the build at state close.
   - Item 121 quotes 1.12 in full, including "não participa do swap normal".
   - Item 122 states the padding of step 3 and the short list of step 4 as CONFIRMADO in the spec. Only the named refusal, when a short list cannot split into the configured groups, is recorded as INFERIDO.
   - `StateSetup.reserve`'s docstring says what it is: the state reserve queue of FORMAT-SPEC load rule 7.

**Tests:**
- A state division's two last clubs go down and the division below's two best go up. The last division swaps with the state reserve as a queue.
- Two seasons of a state: season two's memberships are season one's after the swap, in the order of rule 2.
- Season one's Brazilian fourth is built from season one's state results and contains no club of divisions one to three.
- A short state queue is padded with stateless Brazilian clubs in world order.
- Every season, every club of every state appears in exactly one state division or in the state reserve.

**Commit:** `fix(engine): state divisions carry over, and the Brazilian fourth division comes from this season's states`

### Task 5: a grouped final phase paired inside the group, and qualifiers as data

**Findings closed:**
- Important 6: the grouped final phase is seeded by group instead of by place.
- The truthfulness of items 115 and 120.
- Deferred minor T5: missing tests for the overall table and cross-group leagues.
- The `qualifiers` lambda, which breaks `SeasonState` equality.
- The reuse of `PLAYOFF_ROUNDS` for the final phase's legs.

**Files:** `NationalLeagues.kt`, `Competition.kt`, `StateChampionships.kt`, `Phase.kt` or `Knockout.kt` as needed, tests, `spec/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md` items 115 and 120.

**Rulings:**
1. The FORMAT-SPEC pairing wins over 1.11's parenthetical:
   - FORMAT-SPEC (presets 7 and 10, CONFIRMADO) pairs the first knockout round inside the group, 1st against 2nd, in group order A, B, C, D.
   - Section 1.11 says the grouped league reuses that engine, "emparelhados dentro do próprio grupo nas rodadas iniciais quando aplicável".
   - 1.11's parenthetical, "1o de cada grupo contra o 2o do grupo seguinte", contradicts both, so the FORMAT-SPEC rule wins.

   With q qualifiers per group:
   - The opening rounds stay inside each group, place p against place q+1-p, until one club per group remains.
   - The group winners then meet in bracket order: A against B, C against D, and so on.
   - The better placed club hosts the return leg (FORMAT-SPEC: "o melhor recebe a volta").
   - With `qualifyByOverallTable`, the qualifiers are seeded by the overall table, strong against weak.
2. `Competition.qualifiers` becomes a sealed data type, so that two equal states compare equal. Examples of its cases: per-group top q, overall table top n, a state preset. `SeasonState`'s equality docstring states the property the tests pin.
3. The final phase's legs use their own constant with its own SpecRef, not `PLAYOFF_ROUNDS`.
4. Item 120 records rule 1 and the 1.11 contradiction (INFERIDO for q above 2). Item 115 corrects its claim: the Brazilian fourth division does use a grouped final phase.

**Tests:**
- Four groups of four qualifiers: every first-round tie pairs two clubs of the same group, 1st against 4th and 2nd against 3rd. No club meets a club of another group before one per group remains.
- Two qualifiers per group: 1st against 2nd of the same group, the 1st hosting the return leg.
- `qualifyByOverallTable = true` seeds by the overall table.
- A league with `gamesInsideGroup = false` builds and closes.
- Two `SeasonState`s built from the same seed compare equal, states included.

**Commit:** `fix(engine): a grouped league's final phase pairs inside the group, and qualifiers are data`
